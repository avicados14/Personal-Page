MEGN201 · Design for manufacture

Derby Car

A complete path from dimensioned engineering drawings through fabrication, assembly, and performance validation.

← Back to work

Drawing and tolerancing

I produced detailed GD&T drawings and performed tolerance stack-up analysis before fabrication. This established a clear connection between design intent, manufacturing limits, and the fit of the assembled components.

Build and assembly

I fabricated and assembled the components using the drawings and tolerance plan as the manufacturing reference.

Validation

After assembly, I tested the car against its design specifications and evaluated whether the completed system met the required performance and compliance criteria.
