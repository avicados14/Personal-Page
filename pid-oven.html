PID Easy-Bake Oven

A thermal control project that joined physical fabrication, sensor feedback, and LabVIEW logic in one working system.

← Back to work

The challenge

Design and build a compact oven, then control its temperature accurately enough to complete a real baking task. The project required the physical system and the control strategy to work together rather than treating them as separate exercises.

The approach

I developed a PID controller in LabVIEW to regulate oven temperature from feedback. That meant tuning the controller around the thermal behavior of the build, observing the response, and iterating toward stable performance.

The result

The completed system successfully baked a cookie in approximately eight minutes, demonstrating closed-loop temperature control in a tangible, testable application.
