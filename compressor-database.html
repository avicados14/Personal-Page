Role overview

As a Compressor Engineering Intern, I supported technical-data and mechanical-engineering work. My contributions included a demonstrated data-access prototype, structured reference-data organization, early-stage fluid-system analysis, test-system design support, and technical documentation.

Engineering data access prototype

I designed and demonstrated a lightweight internal prototype that made spreadsheet-derived engineering test records easier to access and review. The workflow transformed records into structured data, handled common data-quality conditions, and supported refresh-oriented updates through a browser-facing interface.

I also evaluated and documented a longer-term modernization direction. This was a prototype and planning effort, not a claim of production deployment or measured business impact.

Structured reference data and request triage

I organized drawing-derived engineering reference information into structured records to support consistent lookup and traceability. I also prototyped a Python-based workflow for identifying relevant analysis requests and associating them with controlled supporting engineering information.

Early-stage fluid-system study

I contributed spreadsheet-based performance and pressure analysis across operating conditions, experimental test-system design support, preliminary component-selection analysis, and sourcing support. The work informed engineering discussion and concept-feasibility planning; it did not represent a final design, completed test campaign, or validated operational result.

Technical foundation and collaboration

I consolidated foundational knowledge in process-diagram conventions, centrifugal-compressor operation, dry-gas-seal concepts, rotating-equipment maintenance, reliability, and safety-oriented operating practices. The internship strengthened my ability to interpret technical reference material, present recommendations at an appropriate level of detail, and collaborate across engineering functions.

Confidentiality

This summary intentionally omits proprietary systems, source records, internal identifiers, engineering values, calculations, supplier information, code, screenshots, and implementation details. No internship files are published or linked.
