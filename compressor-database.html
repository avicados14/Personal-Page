Compressor Test Database

An engineering information project focused on making compressor test data more usable, accurate, and ready for future modernization.

← Back to work

Database redesign

I designed and demonstrated a new Compressor Test Database, improving usability and accuracy while identifying issues in the existing system and proposing modernization to SQL.

Engineering data

I built organized databases for inducers, pump cases, and seal housings to streamline sizing and analysis requests. The work included validating drawings, creating traceable references, creating Item Masters in JDE, and learning to interpret P&IDs.

Broader engineering work

I also supported the EPR 1451 LO Vortex Study through test stand design, load calculations for support cables, pump sizing, and part sourcing. The experience deepened my understanding of fluid mechanics, machine design, compressors, pumps, gearboxes, seals, and bearings.
