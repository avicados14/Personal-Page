Arduino Lockbox

A biometric security system integrating fingerprint input, embedded control, electrical wiring, and a custom mechanical enclosure.

← Back to work

System overview

I programmed and integrated a fingerprint sensor with an Arduino microcontroller to control a physical lock. The project combined circuitry, wiring, embedded logic, and mechanical packaging into one working system.

Mechanical integration

I modeled the housing for the electrical components in SolidWorks, accounting for the relationship between the electronics, the lock mechanism, and the user-facing fingerprint interface.

Documentation

The project archive includes Arduino code, circuit materials, SolidWorks files, and photos of the finished lockbox.
