EDNS151 · Product design

Suitcase Redesign

A usability-focused redesign combining a physical product, rapid prototypes, and a supporting mobile experience.

← Back to work

Design objective

The project asked the team to reconsider how a suitcase could be easier and more useful in everyday travel, then translate that direction into a testable design.

Physical and digital development

I applied CAD modeling and rapid prototyping to the physical redesign and helped develop a mobile application that supported the product's functionality.

Iteration

Design evaluation informed successive improvements to the concept, connecting user needs with the practical limits of form, manufacturing, and interface behavior.
